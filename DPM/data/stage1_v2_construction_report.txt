LABORATORY VARIABLES
----------------------------------------------------------------------
HbA1c (LBXGH) and fasting glucose (LBXGLU) are NOT predictors.
They are used ONLY to construct the training target.
No laboratory column is saved in the dataset.

TARGET (diabetes_risk, 3-class)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
  High     = DIQ010==1 OR HbA1c>=6.5 OR fasting glucose>=126
  Moderate = not High AND (DIQ010==3 OR 5.7<=HbA1c<=6.4 OR 100<=FG<=125)
  Low      = not High/Moderate AND (DIQ010==2 OR >=1 valid lab)
Either lab is used independently (fasting glucose is a subsample).
Undetermined targets are excluded, never assigned arbitrarily.
