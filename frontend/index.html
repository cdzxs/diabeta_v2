DiaBeta — clinical decision support

Diabetes risk assessment, stage by stage.

DiaBeta assists healthcare professionals in evaluating a patient's risk of diabetes using clinical parameters and machine learning. It brings a screener, a detailed risk assessment, and time-based projections together in one connected flow.

Take Assessment

Stage 1

Screener

A quick screen from patient demographics, BMI, and key clinical and lifestyle history — no laboratory values needed. Provides an initial low, moderate, or high risk estimate.

Begin the screener →

Stage 2

Risk Assessment

A complete, higher-level assessment: enter patient information, clinical history, and at least one laboratory value (HbA1c or fasting glucose) for a detailed current risk assessment through clinical rules supported by machine learning. Start here directly, or continue from the Stage 1 screener to carry your answers forward.

Go to Stage 2 →

Stage 3

Prediction Over Time

Projects the assessed profile across 1-year and 2-year perspectives to support earlier, forward-looking clinical review.
