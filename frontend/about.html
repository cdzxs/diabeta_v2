About the tool

A clinical assessment tool for earlier diabetes risk awareness.

This clinical diabetes risk assessment tool uses machine learning and key health indicators to estimate diabetes risk for healthcare professionals.

Start Assessment

What the tool does

This tool combines submitted measurements such as age, BMI, waist circumference, HbA1c, fasting glucose, sex, and race or ethnicity to estimate a diabetes risk tier.

Motivation

Millions of people have diabetes across the world. Early detection helps people seek professional guidance sooner and take practical steps toward better health.

Important disclaimer

This tool is not intended to diagnose any medical condition. Its predictions are based on a machine learning model and cannot substitute professional medical advice, diagnosis, or laboratory testing.
